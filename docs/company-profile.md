# BRIGHT BEAN COFFEE — OVERVIEW

---
## Company Summary
Bright Bean Coffee is a South African high-end coffee manufacturer, distributor, and retailer with a workforce of 81 people. Their operations include bean roasting, national retail, on-site catering, and international export.

* **Headquarters:** Cape Town 
* **Retail Footprint (8 Branches):** Cape Town (3), Johannesburg (2), Durban (2), Port Elizabeth (1)
* **Logistics Depot:** Midrand Hub 
* **Export Markets:** USA, France, Dubai
* **E-Commerce Partner:** CartSmartz Platform
